Versioning behaviour

Active sources are considered by Equipment Research. When a newer source is imported for a vendor, existing active versions are marked Archived.

Archived versions and their product lines remain in the database and their original source files remain on disk. They can be viewed, downloaded, searched explicitly, or reactivated later.
